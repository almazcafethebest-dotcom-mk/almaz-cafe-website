Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what happens to information submitted through this website.

What we collect

If you use the contact form on this site, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide. We don't collect anything else automatically — this site doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

How it's used

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry. It's received and stored via Netlify's form-handling service (the platform this site is hosted on) and is not sold, shared, or used for marketing.

A note on your browser's local storage

This site remembers your choice of colour theme (Light, Dark, or Vibrant) using your browser's local storage, so it stays set next time you visit. This is stored only on your own device, is not sent to us, and contains no personal information.

Third-party services

This site links out to Uber Eats and DoorDash for online ordering, and embeds a Google Maps view of our location — each of those services has its own privacy policy governing any information you provide directly to them.

Questions

For any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at (09) 622 2108 or via the Contact & Location page.
