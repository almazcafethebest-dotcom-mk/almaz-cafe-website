Support & frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most. Can't find yours? Use the Ask Almaz chat in the bottom-right corner, or call us directly.

What are your opening hours?

We're open every day, 11:00am – 9:00pm, including most public holidays unless posted otherwise on the door.

Is the food halal?

Yes, our meat is halal.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes — dishes like samosas, spanakopita, hummus and falafel are vegetarian, and several can be made vegan. Ask your server if you have specific dietary needs.

Can I bring my own wine (BYO)?

Yes, we're BYO.

Do you deliver, or offer takeaway?

Both. You can order delivery or pickup through Uber Eats or DoorDash, or call ahead on (09) 622 2108 for takeaway.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes — free street parking right outside on Onehunga Mall.

Is the cafe wheelchair accessible?

Yes, we have wheelchair-accessible entry and seating.

Can you cater for large groups or private functions?

Yes, we can accommodate groups and private functions of up to around 40 people. Call (09) 622 2108 ahead of time to arrange it.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit cards, debit cards, and contactless/NFC payments.

What's the price range?

Most people spend around $15–25 per person.

Do you take reservations?

For everyday dining, it's walk-in — for bigger groups or private functions, call ahead on (09) 622 2108.

What kind of food do you serve?

Turkish, Middle Eastern and Mediterranean dishes, plus a few Persian specials like lamb pasendeh — see the full menu.
