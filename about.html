Our story

Named for a diamond,
run like family.

Almaz Cafe has been a fixture on Onehunga Mall for over twenty years — a family-run kitchen where the couple behind the counter still make almost everything fresh, every single day.

Why "Almaz"

Almaz means diamond — a name chosen to reflect the standard behind every plate. Regulars often mention the same thing: the couple who run the cafe are warm, attentive, and proud of food they make themselves rather than buy in.

A local institution

Long before "cozy neighbourhood cafe" was a trend, Almaz was already the go-to on Onehunga Mall — one family has kept coming back for around twenty years, and it's a regular Sunday-night stop for many more.

How we work

Made fresh, daily

Kebabs, sides and Persian specials are prepared in-house every day, not shipped in.

Open, welcoming room

Big front windows that open onto Onehunga Mall bring the street in — a favourite spot on a warm evening.

Everyone's welcome
