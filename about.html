Our story

Named for a diamond,
run like family.

Almaz Cafe has been a fixture on Onehunga Mall for over twenty years — a family-run kitchen where the couple behind the counter still make almost everything fresh, every single day.

Almaz/ɑːlˈmɑːz/ — noun

Diamond. Chosen as the cafe's name to set a standard: every plate should be something worth coming back for.

Inside Almaz Cafe

Why "Almaz"

Regulars often mention the same thing: the couple who run the cafe are warm, attentive, and proud of food they make themselves rather than buy in.

A local institution

Long before "cozy neighbourhood cafe" was a trend, Almaz was already the go-to on Onehunga Mall — one family has kept coming back for around twenty years, and it's a regular Sunday-night stop for many more.

How we work

Made fresh, daily
