Ring ahead

Skip the wait,
skip the app fee

You don’t need a delivery app or an account. Ring the shop, say what you want and roughly when, and it’s off the grill as you arrive — to take away, or to sit down and eat.

  • No service fee, no delivery fee
  • Menu price is the price you pay
  • Cooked to order, not sitting in a bag
Call (09) 622 2108

Open Daily, 11am–9pm

  1. 1

    Call the shop

    One of the owners answers. Tell them the dish and how you want it — sauces, spice, salad or no salad.

  2. 2

    Say when you’ll be there

    Fifteen minutes is plenty for most things. Rice meals and the Persian casseroles are worth twenty.

  3. 3

    It’s cooked when you arrive

    Nothing sits under a lamp. The grill goes on for your order, so it’s hot when it reaches you.

  4. 4

    Walk in and pay

    Card, contactless or cash at the counter. Free street parking right outside, and a table if you’d rather eat in.
